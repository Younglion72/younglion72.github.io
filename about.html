About CoverPoint

Real guidance from a real licensed agent — not a call center.

Hi, I'm Ahmed Taylor.

I'm a licensed insurance agent based in Georgia, and I started CoverPoint for a simple reason: insurance is something everyone needs, but almost nobody feels confident about. The industry is full of jargon, fine print, and pressure tactics — and I think you deserve better.

My approach is education first. Before we ever talk about a policy, I want you to actually understand what you're buying and why. That's what this site is for — the articles, FAQs, and resources here are free for everyone, whether you ever become a client or not.

And if you're outside my licensed states, I'll still try to help — at minimum, I'll point you toward trustworthy resources, and when possible, toward another licensed professional in your area.

What I Believe

  • Education before sales. An informed client makes better decisions — and keeps their coverage longer.
  • No pressure, ever. If the honest answer is "you don't need that policy," that's the answer you'll get.
  • The right coverage, not the most coverage. Insurance should fit your life and budget, not an agent's quota.
  • Honesty about limitations. I'm licensed in GA, TX, SC, MS, and TN. If you're elsewhere, I'll tell you upfront and help you find someone who can serve you.

How I Work

Every conversation starts with your situation — your family, health, budget, and goals. From there, we compare options across multiple carriers so you're choosing from a real market, not a single company's menu. And after you're covered, I'm still around at renewal time, when life changes, or when you just have a question.

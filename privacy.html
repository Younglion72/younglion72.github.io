Privacy Policy

How your information is collected, used, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you use this website, we may collect:

  • Information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, phone number, state, and any details you share through the contact form or by email.
  • Information collected automatically — basic technical data such as browser type, device, pages visited, and referring site, collected through standard server logs or analytics tools (if enabled).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide requested information or quotes
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your inquiry (only with your consent)
  • To connect you with a licensed professional in your state when we cannot serve you directly
  • To improve the content and performance of this website

3. SMS and Phone Communications

If you provide your phone number and consent to be contacted, you may receive calls or text messages from CoverPoint regarding your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Consent to be contacted is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only in these cases:

  • With insurance carriers or partners as needed to provide quotes or services you request
  • With a licensed insurance professional in your state, if you ask for a referral and we cannot serve you directly
  • With service providers who help operate this website (e.g., form processing), under their own privacy obligations
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process

5. Affiliate Links and Third-Party Sites

This website may contain links to third-party websites, including affiliate or partner links. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase or complete an application, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Third-party sites have their own privacy practices, which we encourage you to review. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external sites.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you provide. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing info@getcoverpoint.com
  • You may opt out of communications at any time by replying STOP to texts or clicking unsubscribe in emails

8. Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
